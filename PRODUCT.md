# PRODUCT.md

## Register
**brand** - the marketing and content surfaces are the product's first impression. Articles are long-form content, the home and membership pages are persuasion. Design IS the product here.

## Product purpose
OraDiscuss sells a recurring membership of **read-only Oracle DBA tooling**. Every pack runs on the customer's own machine and produces two artifacts from one collection: a **report a human reads** and a **briefing the customer's own AI reads**. Nothing is uploaded, ever.

Positioning line: **"Your scripts collect. Your AI reasons. Your data never leaves."**

Catalog (prices are read from D1, never hardcoded): free DBA First Aid Kit; Toolkit $99/yr launch, $149 regular; Toolkit + Academy $199/yr launch, $249 regular. Launch price locked for life while continuously subscribed. Annual only.

## Users
Working Oracle DBAs, mid-career to senior, running 19c through 26ai on Exadata, RAC, on-prem and OCI estates. Two generations served by one artifact:
- **The Classic DBA** reads the HTML report. Sceptical of marketing, has seen every vendor promise.
- **The AI DBA** feeds `briefing.json` to their own Claude or GPT. Under a "use AI" mandate at work and often forbidden from pasting production data into a chatbot.

They buy on a personal card, without procurement. They are not decision makers for enterprise purchases and must never be addressed as if they were.

## Brand personality
Precise. Unhurried. Load-bearing.

Three voice words, as physical objects: a **calibration certificate**, an **instrument panel in a control room**, a **well-set technical manual from a national lab**. Confidence comes from evidence and restraint, never from enthusiasm.

The author is Mahmoud Darwish, Oracle ACE Associate, roughly nineteen years on production estates. The credential is a **bio fact only, never a product claim**. The trust line that does the work: *"Every script here ran on real production estates by a working DBA before it was published."*

## Anti-references
- **The AI-era SaaS landing page.** Purple-to-orange gradient text, frosted glass on every surface, a hero metric template. The audience has seen a hundred of these attached to vapour; looking like one spends credibility we cannot afford.
- **Oracle's own corporate marketing.** We are the independent practitioner, not the vendor.
- **The 2015 Oracle blog.** Dense unstyled text, sidebar widgets, no point of view.
- **Enterprise trust theatre.** Stock photos of people pointing at monitors, invented logos, fake testimonials.
- **Countdown timers, fake scarcity, invented statistics.** Absolutely banned. The launch price is honest and its rise is pre-announced.

## Strategic design principles
1. **Evidence over adjectives.** Show the real artifact. A screenshot of an actual report outperforms any sentence about quality.
2. **The free tier is the demo of paid quality.** It must look and feel like the best thing on the site.
3. **Read-only is a feature, and it is visible.** Say it where a cautious DBA looks first.
4. **Never state a verdict.** The product reports what the database recorded; the site's voice matches that discipline.
5. **No em dashes anywhere.** Founder rule, applies to UI copy and CSS comments alike.
6. **Every price comes from the database.** No page hardcodes money.
7. **Motion must mean something.** It dramatizes the product (a report filling in, a field breathing) or it does not ship.
